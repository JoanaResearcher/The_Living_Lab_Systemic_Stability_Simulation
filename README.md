# The Living Lab: Systemic Stability Simulation
## The Living Lab: Simulación de Estabilidad Sistémica

**Lead Technical Researcher:** Joana Camp, Old Dominion University  
**Academic Profile:** B.S. Occupational & Technical Studies | GPA: 3.95  
**Research Core:** The Living Lab Series

---

### Executive Summary | Resumen Ejecutivo
**EN:** The Systemic Stability Simulation is a professional, character-driven instructional tool developed to visualize findings from a 28-year ethnographic study. Utilizing **Human Performance Technology (HPT)** and **Universal Design for Learning (UDL)**, this tool demonstrates how inclusive, accessible administrative pathways reduce the "Bandwidth Tax" on families and stabilize federal housing outcomes.

**ES:** La Simulación de Estabilidad Sistémica es una herramienta instructiva profesional centrada en personajes, desarrollada para visualizar los hallazgos de un estudio etnográfico de 28 años. Utilizando la **Tecnología de Desempeño Humano (HPT)** y el **Diseño Universal para el Aprendizaje (UDL)**, esta herramienta demuestra cómo las rutas administrativas inclusivas y accesibles reducen el "Impuesto de Ancho de Banda" en las familias y estabilizan los resultados de la vivienda federal.

---

### Universal Design Features | Características de Diseño Universal

* **Intersectional Representation:** Features diverse administrative profiles—including Veterans, Seniors, and Bilingual families—to model systemic success for all citizens.
* **Linguistic Equity:** Professional-grade Spanish/English toggle integrated across all narrative scenarios and UI controls.
* **Accessible Navigation:** Optimized for high readability, disability access, and user-controlled pacing to prevent cognitive overwhelm.
* **Operational Impact Dashboard:** Real-time metrics quantifying Administrative Efficiency, Community Trust, and Housing Stability.

---

### Research Objectives | Objetivos de Investigación

1. **Quantify the Bandwidth Tax:** Measure the cognitive load of standard administrative friction versus HPT-informed solutions.
2. **Mitigate Systemic Error:** Navigate the critical 15-day housing window using "Job Aids" that solve the 30% administrative error rate.
3. **Model Stability:** Prove that user-centered, accessible design leads to measurable increases in long-term housing stability.
